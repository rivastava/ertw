# Code of conduct

ERTW contributors are expected to communicate professionally, welcome
good-faith questions, critique ideas and code rather than people, disclose
conflicts of interest, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or the
publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
community. Reports should be sent privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile or a private repository security/advisory channel. Reports will
be reviewed in confidence, with conflicts of interest recused where practical.
